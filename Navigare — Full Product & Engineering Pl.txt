Navigare — Full Product & Engineering PlanFrom Python/Streamlit Prototype → Production Web App for Local Retail OwnersThe Problem We're SolvingSmall business owners spend 30–50% of their time on admin — tracking finances, managing inventory, making routine decisions — instead of doing the work that actually earns revenue. They have no dedicated ops staff, no analytics tools built for their scale, and no time to learn enterprise software like Shopify or Square.The result: they manage their stores blindly. No dashboards, no trend signals, no early warnings. This is why local businesses close at high rates while corporations absorb their market share.Navigare's job is to be the ops person they can't afford to hire.Delivery Strategy — Hybrid of All Three ApproachesYour proposal evaluated three delivery modes.

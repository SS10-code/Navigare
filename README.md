ReadMe: 

Empowering small business owners to spend less time on admin and more time growing their business. 

Problem Statement: Small business owners spend 30-50% of their time on administrative operations, tracking finances, managing paperwork, organizing inventory, and making routine operational decisions, rather than focusing on their main work (making products, serving clients, growing revenue).  

Without access to dedicated operations staff of simple analytics tools, many owners are forced to manage their stores blindly. Lacking actionable insights, these small businesses face disproportionately high closure rates, threatening local economic independence and opening the door for corporate domains.


Solutions & Impact
Our platform provides intuitive visual analytics and support designed specifically for non-technical small business owners. 

Immediate impact: 
Unified Business Dashboard: Real-time visibility into sales, expenses, and  key metrics (buying inventory e.g.) 
Data-Driven Operations: Actionable insights to guide inventory purchases and daily routine decisions without needing dedicated staff 

Future impact:
Profitability Insights: Algorithmic suggestions to optimize and reduce waste 
Automatic Predictive Forecasting: Machine learning models (XGBoost e.g. ) to forecast demand, cash flow, and stock needs before shortage
